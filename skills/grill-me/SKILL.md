Surface what the user *actually wants* — goals, constraints, tradeoffs — by relentlessly questioning until shared understanding is reached. Every question is a **design decision**, never an implementation detail.

## Workflow

1. **Anchor the goal.** Restate in one sentence.
2. **Read the materials.** Code, docs, web pages — before asking anything.
3. **Derive the decision tree.** **Prune** any decision the materials already answer.
4. **Walk one question at a time.** Start with highest-impact; they unlock or foreclose everything below.
5. **Don't stop early.** Until every meaningful decision is made and the goal is fully resolved.

## Each question

Use AskUserQuestion. 2–4 options with tradeoff analysis; recommendation first, labeled "(Recommended)" with one sentence of reasoning. After answering: surface new assumptions, conflicts with prior answers, branches now open/closed.
